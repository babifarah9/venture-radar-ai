# VentureRadar AI — 2–3 minute demo script

## 0:00–0:20 — Problem

“Founders can spend weeks searching for demand signals, competitors, and customer pain—then still struggle to decide whether an idea deserves a pilot. VentureRadar AI turns one market question into an evidence-backed launch decision.”

Show the empty workspace and the integration label.

## 0:20–0:40 — Ask the question

Use: **Could there be a commercial market for private 5G networks for warehouses in Africa?**

“VentureRadar runs live research across demand, competitors, and adoption barriers using SerpApi.”

Click **Analyze opportunity**.

## 0:40–1:20 — Explain the intelligence

“The result starts with an executive readout grounded in the retrieved evidence. The score is not a black box: demand, white space, timing, and feasibility remain visible. We can immediately see the market signals, likely competitors, and four concrete gaps.”

Scroll through the executive readout, scorecard, competitors, and gaps. Point out the **AI + Live research** badge and open one evidence source.

## 1:20–1:50 — Turn research into a venture

“VentureRadar does not stop at a market summary. It proposes a beachhead customer, business model, and defensible edge, then converts the thesis into a testable eight-week plan.”

Show the recommended venture and launch plan. Emphasize the proof gates.

## 1:50–2:15 — Name.com integration

“A launchable concept also needs a viable identity. Name.com is part of the core workflow: VentureRadar searches brand terms, checks availability, and returns live first-year pricing—not a static list of invented domains.”

Show the domain shortlist, availability badges, and pricing.

## 2:15–2:35 — Reliability and close

“Every integration fails independently. If a provider is unavailable, VentureRadar labels the fallback mode clearly so the workflow stays demonstrable without pretending mock evidence is live.”

“VentureRadar AI helps founders find the signal before the crowd—and turn that signal into the fastest responsible experiment.”

End on the full report with the live URL visible.

## Recording checklist

- Record at 1080p with browser zoom around 90–100%.
- Confirm the badge says **AI + Live research** before recording.
- Confirm Name.com results display availability and pricing.
- Keep the final video below three minutes.
- Do not expose API keys, browser bookmarks, notifications, or personal tabs.
